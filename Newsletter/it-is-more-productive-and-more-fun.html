It Is More Productive, and It Is More Fun

Lesson 5 of How to Become Your Own Teacher: Learning How to Learn is finished. Read it, watch it, listen to it, page through the slides, or see the whole lesson on one page.

The single largest cause of failure in teaching yourself is not difficulty. It is stopping. Almost nothing worth learning fits into one sitting, so a mediocre method you will actually use beats an optimal method you will abandon in three weeks.

And no single method wins. Each one buys you something at a price, and each one has its own way of lying to you. The gain is in running several on purpose, so that their blind spots cancel out. The lesson walks through one sequence:
 
1 · Read Tests recognition

Get the map: the vocabulary, the main ideas and the shape of the territory.
 
2 · Build Tests application

Make something small to find out where the map is wrong, and what the book skipped.
 
3 · Ask Sharpens the question

Take the wall you hit to someone else. By now your question is specific, which is the only kind that gets a useful answer.
 
4 · Explain Tests understanding

Teach it, or write it down as though you were. Every gap you glossed over shows up at once.

Recognition, application and understanding are different kinds of knowing. You can pass the first and fail the other two, which is exactly the trap most self-teachers fall into.

The lesson also covers the comfort trap, the freedoms you do not get inside a school, and why fun is not a luxury but the mechanism. Over five years, the person who enjoys their method simply puts in far more hours, and hours are what learning is made of.

There is no best method, only the mix that fits you. Effectiveness and enjoyment are the same decision.
