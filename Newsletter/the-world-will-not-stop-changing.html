The World Will Not Stop Changing

Lesson 3 of How to Become Your Own Teacher: Learning How to Learn is finished. Read it, watch it, listen to it, page through the slides, or see the whole lesson on one page.

The only thing that will not change is that the world will change. You do not get a vote on this, and neither does anyone teaching you.

Everything you learn comes with an expiry date, and not all of it expires at the same speed.
 
Principles Last decades

How to break a large problem into smaller ones. How to tell a real cause from a coincidence.
 
Practices Last years

How work is organised, and which trade-offs your industry currently accepts.
 
Tools Last months

The specific product, the specific version, the specific button.

Most people invest their learning time in exact reverse order. Tools feel urgent, so they get the attention. Principles feel abstract, so they get skipped. Then the tool is replaced, and the effort goes with it.

The rest of the lesson is about re-learning, the harder skill underneath all of this. It covers why re-learning costs more than learning something for the first time, how deep expertise can become a liability when a field shifts, and three habits that keep you ahead of the change.

A diploma is a snapshot of what you knew once. The ability to relearn is the thing that keeps renewing it.
