# There Is No Best Way to Learn, Only a Better One: image generation

Generated on 2026-11-12 using the built-in `image_gen` tool. The three landscape
illustrations are connected to the lesson's Reading tab. The square hero is a
separate cover asset for cards and social sharing.

All final assets are PNGs in `public/images/lessons/own-teacher/`. Landscape images
are 1600×900; the square cover is 1024×1024. Generated originals were resized with
Sharp, preserving the complete composition, and saved as optimized indexed PNGs.
Existing lesson alt text and captions match the generated scenes.

## Exact prompts

For each image, the final prompt is the shared style below followed immediately by
that image's composition and scene prompt. No reference images were sent to the
generation tool; adjacent course illustrations were inspected to match their style.

### Shared style

```text
Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: a finished educational course illustration for the lesson 'There Is No Best Way to Learn, Only a Better One'.
Style/medium: flat vector editorial illustration with crisp clean geometric shapes and restrained off-white linework, matching an existing course series. Deep navy #1a1a2e background, warm orange #ff6b35 accents, warm off-white #f5f0dc shapes and linework. Use solid colors and very subtle paper grain. No photorealism, no 3D, no glossy gradients. Quiet, balanced, practical, optimistic mood. Generous negative space and clear silhouettes that remain understandable on a mobile screen.
Constraints: NO text, lettering, words, numbers, labels, logos, signatures or watermarks anywhere. No ranking, trophies, winning method, or learning-style categories.
```

### no-best-way-hero.png

```text
Composition/framing: wide landscape 16:9 illustration, ideally 1600x900. One unified scene with comfortable margins.
Scene: A single simple off-white human figure with a small warm orange backpack stands at the point where THREE terrains meet: a smooth road, open water, and a steep rocky slope. Place a bicycle on the road, a small sailboat on the water, and a clear coil of climbing rope WITH an ice axe at the foot of the rocky slope. Each terrain is visually distinct and connected around the same learner, rather than separate unrelated scenes. Bicycle, sailboat, and climbing equipment must have equal visual weight and similar display scale; none is privileged, crossed out, glowing, or highlighted above the others. The image conveys that each tool suits its own ground. Calm balanced composition with all three sets of equipment fully visible. No extra characters or vehicles.
```

### no-best-way-three-fields.png

```text
Composition/framing: wide landscape 16:9 illustration, ideally 1600x900, divided into THREE evenly sized side-by-side panels with subtle off-white separators and equal visual weight.
Scene: The SAME simplified adult learner appears in all three panels with identical short dark hair, warm off-white face, and orange top. LEFT: the learner sits comfortably in an armchair reading an open book, with a short stack of newsletter envelopes beside the chair and a clearly visible pair of headphones. CENTRE: the same learner sits at a desk watching a laptop whose screen shows only a simple triangular play symbol; a small half-built model is on the desk beside it, conveying learning then building. RIGHT: the same learner stands outdoors beside a small market stall with visible repair patches and a rebuilt wooden counter, naturally shaking hands with another person, conveying business through practice and conversation. The second person is only in the right panel. Keep the learner's identity consistent. Same learner, different tools and setting. All book pages, envelopes, screens and stall signage must be blank except the triangular play symbol. Do not include written labels or letter-like decoration.
```

### no-best-way-seven-methods.png

```text
Composition/framing: wide landscape 16:9 illustration, ideally 1600x900. Exactly SEVEN distinct small objects, evenly spaced along one gentle shallow arc with comfortable side margins, equal size and equal visual weight. No people.
Scene: Reading left to right, show (1) an open book with blank pages, (2) a small video screen showing only a simple triangular play symbol, (3) a pair of over-ear headphones, (4) two overlapping speech bubbles treated as ONE object group with blank interiors, (5) a hammer, (6) a small blank classroom chalkboard on a stand, (7) a cracked ceramic cup clearly repaired with ONE warm-orange kintsugi-like seam. A single thin orange thread runs visibly from one object to the next beneath the objects, connecting ALL seven, then ends in a small neat knot AFTER the last cup. Use off-white objects with minimal orange details; the connecting thread and repair seam are orange. Keep every icon distinct and countable; do not add extra books, tools, cups, shapes resembling eighth icons, stars or decorative objects. This represents seven valid methods connected into one personal mix, with none ranked above the others.
```
